How it works
Words are drawn with crypto.getRandomValues, never Math.random, and nothing leaves this page — there is no network request, no analytics and no storage of generated passwords. Your settings (not your passwords) are kept in this browser's local storage so the page opens configured the way you left it.

Substitutions are predictable to an attacker, so they add far less strength than they look like they do. The real strength here comes from word count and length: prefer three or four words over a heavier substitution level.
